## A reproducible 2–3 minute path

1. Open the canonical URL in a fresh browser and choose **Explore a fictional example**. Today shows saved sources, pending reviews, next steps and a visit. State that these are synthetic records stored in this browser.
2. Choose **Compare the sources** for the medication difference. Show both original Lisinopril quotations and their sources. Explain that the software leaves the discrepancy unresolved; it does not choose a dose or recommend treatment.
3. Open **Review**, inspect an original quote, and accept one non-medication pending detail. Show it in **My care** with its source link. Explain that acceptance records the patient's review, not clinical truth.
4. Use **+ Add an update** and save a titled fictional note such as `Task: request the fictional clinic visit summary`. Its proposal begins pending. Review it, then open **Follow-through**, update a personal check-back date or save an outreach draft. No clinic message is sent by the website.
5. Open **Visits → Prepare / edit / recap** to show the agenda/questions. A saved recap can be captured as a source for normal review. Do not claim it schedules a real appointment or imports an EHR.
6. Show **Care brief** preview and **Settings → Encrypted backup**. Explain that browser records do not sync automatically; a backup is needed to move devices/domains or recover after clearing storage. The browser vault itself is not encrypted at rest by the website; the exported file is encrypted.

Optional short document demonstration: **Sources → Add a source**, choose the checked-in `web/tests/fixtures/fictional-note.pdf` or `.png`, inspect the locally extracted text, save it, then show its pending proposals and page links. OCR is English-only and must be checked for names, numbers and units. This can replace a longer visit/backup segment to keep the video within the official duration.

## Claims to keep narrow

The product organizes source-linked reported care information, explicit review and practical follow-through. It does not diagnose, prescribe, triage, choose a clinical winner, provide full EHR/FHIR interoperability, automatically transcribe speech, sync patient records across devices, or revoke a PDF/text copy after sharing. Physical microphone and broader device verification must remain scoped to actual evidence.
