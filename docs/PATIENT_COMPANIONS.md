# CareInOne patient companions

This pass makes saved information useful during a real conversation and while arranging care. These are local organizational tools. They do not select clinical instructions, infer that care happened, send messages automatically, or require a model subscription.

## Patient outcomes and acceptance criteria

| ID | Patient outcome | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Start without rebuilding a medical chart | An empty patient can start an appointment companion directly from Today or Visits; no known appointment date is required. |
| P2 | Explain what matters quickly | An editable personal opening and ranked top three questions create a compact clinician-facing brief; additional questions remain separate. Read aloud and share the exact brief. |
| P3 | Ask the right questions | Copy chosen saved questions, unresolved discrepancies, and follow-through questions into an editable agenda, with supporting sources and snapshot labeling. Reorder and remove items. |
| P4 | Stay present in the conversation | One-question-at-a-time visit mode saves answers, discussed/ask-later state and the current position, with recovery after relaunch. |
| P5 | Keep answers without entering them twice | Save answers as original patient-reported evidence; generated question prompts are excluded from extraction. Normal review is required before any care claim changes. |
| P6 | Get unstuck on an open task | Choose an obstacle and next action; prepare an editable source-grounded clinic message or call script; copy/share without pretending it was delivered. |
| P7 | Stop remembering when to chase a reply | Save a personal check-back date separately from clinical due dates. Today surfaces it; optional generic local notifications honor consent and task relevance. |
| P8 | Know what happened after reaching out | Save actual outreach history and reply drafts. Replies enter the evidence/review pipeline; organizational actions do not advance clinical stages. |
| P9 | Resume easily | Today continues an in-progress visit before a preparation draft, displays personal check-backs, and links follow-through directly to practical help. |
| P10 | Trust that drafts and deletions behave predictably | New metadata persists in the existing protected local store and encrypted backup. Legacy stores remain readable. Failed saves preserve the prior committed state. Source deletion removes associated copied data; separately preserved evidence remains. |

## Boundaries

- The companion organizes what the patient wants to ask and remembers what they enter. Marking a question “discussed,” finishing the visit screen, reaching out, or sharing a message does not verify a clinical instruction or confirm delivery. Only the explicit patient-reported completion action marks a care task completed by the patient's report; it does not create a test result or settle conflicting instructions.
- Visit answers and actual follow-through replies become preserved, patient-reported evidence. Generated agenda questions and outgoing message prompts are excluded from the extracted answer text. Saving a reply does not automatically update My Care: extraction proposals still require normal validation and patient review. Local extraction supports explicit patterns, not every natural-language answer; the original text and manual source-linked entry remain available.
- Personal check-back dates are separate from clinical due dates. They never change the date in a saved instruction, establish medical urgency, or imply that care was missed. Future reminders require both notification consent and task-reminder relevance. A helper whose underlying task is paused, completed, or dismissed does not schedule an active-task check-back reminder.
- Editing source text or its event date may temporarily retire reviewed claims and remove a derived task while review is pending. The follow-through helper keeps its message, replies, personal check-back time, and outreach history in a **paused plan** as long as its source evidence exists. The patient must choose a current reviewed task to reconnect it. Reconnection preserves the patient's drafts and history; it does not merge two helper plans, select a clinical winner, or silently refresh an old message. Review the draft against the current sources before using it.
- Source deletion is different from source editing. Deleting any source referenced by a follow-through helper removes that entire helper and its copied messages, local reply drafts, contact notes, and recovery draft. A removed helper cannot be restored by returning to an older navigation screen or shared from that screen. For visit companions, deletion removes source-linked copied questions and their answers; deleting a captured answer source also clears retained answer copies. Independently preserved reply evidence survives removal of a helper unless that evidence source itself is deleted. Existing exported backups and copies already shared outside the app are independent and cannot be recalled.
- Successfully saved drafts, answers, question position, and outreach history persist in the protected local store and encrypted backup. A failed disk write leaves the previous committed state intact. The follow-through helper pauses automatic retries, displays Retry/Discard controls, guards its Back action, and retains unsaved edits in the current app session when changing tabs. The live visit editor keeps a failed answer in its open editor and guards question changes, source navigation, and closing actions until saving succeeds. **Session memory is not durable storage:** unsaved edits can be lost if the app process terminates. Retry successfully before force-quitting; reopening after a successful save should recover the durable draft.
- Synthetic practice and real visit notes must remain distinguishable. Turn off Synthetic practice for a real visit. A question copied from synthetic evidence keeps its synthetic attribution, including its answers; a single capture that mixes real answers with synthetic-question answers is rejected rather than silently classifying real patient notes as demo data. Use a separate real companion or remove the synthetic answer before capture. Follow-through replies have an explicit synthetic-information toggle that must match the text being entered. Review demo labels on all outgoing copies.
- Copying and sharing are explicit patient actions. The helper does not send a clinic message, call a provider, verify a contact address, or infer delivery from opening the share sheet. Drafts and source-grounded prompts may contain sensitive information; inspect the exact text and destination first. Record “I reached out” only after doing so.
- Read aloud uses the system speech synthesizer after an explicit tap; no visit recording or ambient listening starts. The app stops its speech when leaving the brief, changing its content, or leaving the active scene. Installed language resources, offline playback, audio interruptions, Bluetooth routing, and lock/background behavior still require physical-device validation; a simulator test does not establish those properties on an iPhone.
- Physical-device recovery, local speech, notification delivery, accessibility with assistive technology, and patient usability studies are tracked separately from automated correctness tests. These workflows have not been established as medically validated or superior through patient trials.
