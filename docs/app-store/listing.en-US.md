## Description

Your care does not happen in one place. It arrives in appointments, documents, conversations, portal messages, and the things you remember afterward. CareInOne helps you keep the thread.

Start with one useful update. Type or paste a note, photograph a document, import a PDF, or record a recap. Preserve the source, review the details, and connect them with what you have already saved.

KNOW WHAT NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION
See upcoming appointments, unfinished next steps, recent changes, and reports that need clarification. Each item stays connected to the information behind it.

WALK INTO VISITS PREPARED
Build a short opening, choose your most important questions, and keep answers as the conversation unfolds. Return to your appointment companion without losing your place.

CARRY THE CONTEXT FORWARD
Choose the information you want in a visit packet and share it as text or PDF. Keep a copy of a handoff and see when its supporting information changes. Prepare an editable clinic message, record your own outreach, and set a personal date to check back.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL STORY
Review medications, allergies, measurements, and other reported details alongside their source history. Compare conflicting reports without silently replacing one with another. Search your saved sources, questions, notes, and reviewed information.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR INFORMATION
Capture, local text recognition, review, search, and care organization work without a cloud connection. Optional on-device transcription depends on your device and language. Protect access with device authentication and create a passphrase-protected encrypted backup that you control.

Cloud analysis is optional and requires a configured gateway, permission for its displayed model providers, and service availability. It proposes details from source text; you decide what to accept. Original photos, PDFs, and audio are not uploaded by the app's cloud-analysis flow.

No complete medical history or live connection to a health-record system is required. CareInOne is free, with no advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

CareInOne organizes reported information. It does not diagnose, prescribe, determine which conflicting medical instruction is correct, or monitor emergencies. Check important details against their sources and discuss medical decisions with your treating care team.
